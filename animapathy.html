Theory & research

Animapathy

A developing framework for investigating whether some people experience suffering, responsibility, and duty to care in ways that are comparatively less bounded by species.

Early conceptual development
Why this work exists

A question worth taking seriously.

Animal caregivers can experience substantial physical, psychological, financial, occupational, social, and temporal costs while the seriousness of that caregiving role remains comparatively under-recognized.

Animapathy is the working name for a developing line of inquiry into whether species membership alone is sufficient to explain how people experience caregiving responsibility, moral obligation, burden, grief, and decision-making.

This work is still being developed. The conceptual model, research questions, and future measurement work are being approached deliberately and are not yet presented as an established psychological construct.
