Dignified Paw

A developing, psychologically informed approach to navigating serious illness, disability, changing care needs, ethical conflict, end-of-life decisions, death care, and remembrance.

In development
The purpose

Make room for both love and difficulty.

Caregiving decisions are rarely only medical. They can involve suffering, uncertainty, values, disability, finances, family dynamics, practical limits, grief, hope, guilt, responsibility, and the wish to preserve dignity throughout.

Dignified Paw is being developed as a way to help caregivers think through those decisions in a manner that is individualized to the whole animal and the person responsible for their care.

The decision-support model and practical tools are still being developed. This page is intentionally limited while that work takes shape.
While we build
